# Wave Solution Topical Authority Blueprint

This document is a white-hat SEO, semantic search, CRO, and local search roadmap for turning Wave Solution into a durable Gold Coast market leader. It is designed to align with Google's Helpful Content guidance, title-link guidance, structured-data guidance, and Google Business Profile naming and category rules.

The strategy is intentionally long-term:

- Build genuinely useful pages for real Gold Coast customers.
- Use local specificity instead of thin suburb templates.
- Strengthen trust, proof, clarity, and conversion pathways.
- Expand topical depth through service silos, helpful resources, and E-E-A-T assets.
- Avoid shortcuts that create thin, duplicate, or over-optimized pages.

## Current Site Reality

What already exists or is now in place:

- A cleaner Gold Coast-first homepage with stronger trust messaging and service routing.
- Core cleaning service landing pages for house cleaning, bond cleaning, end of lease cleaning, office cleaning, commercial cleaning, and deep cleaning.
- Newly added core pages for carpet cleaning and pest control.
- A blog hub and existing article routes.
- A sitemap that includes the main service cluster.
- Stronger cleaning-first metadata, schema, and internal linking foundations.

What still needs the next wave of execution:

- Supporting silo pages under each main service category.
- Unique suburb pages for priority Gold Coast suburbs.
- A stronger content library built around real customer questions.
- Better E-E-A-T assets including team profiles, author bylines, case studies, and proof.
- More mature internal links between services, suburbs, and articles.
- Real review acquisition and GBP activity at a consistent monthly pace.

- Use a hub-and-silo model that lets Google understand service depth and lets users move quickly from broad intent to specific booking intent.

Recommended top-level structure:

- `/services`
- `/blog`
- `/locations`
- `/about`
- `/contact`

### Non-Negotiable Rules

- Do not use a single reusable text template with suburb names swapped.
- Research each suburb's property mix, pace, and cleaning pain points.
- Mention real local landmarks only when they improve usefulness.
- Use suburb-specific FAQs that reflect actual conditions.
- Embed the Google Map for the service area or relevant business location.
- Link back to the house-cleaning pillar, booking page, and nearby suburb pages.

## SECTION 5 - On-Page SEO Structure

### Title Formula

- Primary keyword + benefit or trust signal + brand

Examples:

- `House Cleaning Gold Coast | Trusted Local Cleaners | Wave Solution`
- `Bond Cleaning Gold Coast | Inspection-Ready Move-Out Cleaning | Wave Solution`
- `Pest Control Gold Coast | Local Treatments for Homes and Businesses | Wave Solution`

### Meta Description Formula

- 150 to 160 characters
- Include the service, one trust or value signal, and a CTA

Example:

- `Trusted local house cleaning across the Gold Coast. Fully insured team, flexible bookings, and fast quotes. Request your local clean today.`

### Heading Layout

- H1: primary keyword
- H2: service benefits
- H2: what's included
- H2: pricing guide
- H2: why choose us
- H2: FAQs
- H2: service areas

### Important Note

FAQ schema can still be used to clarify page meaning, but Google currently limits FAQ rich results to well-known government and health sites. Use it for structure and machine clarity, not as a guaranteed SERP feature.

- cleaning services

- home sanitation
- housekeeping services
- vacate cleaning
- move-out cleaning
- janitorial services
- commercial hygiene
- property maintenance cleaning
- disinfection services
- rental handover cleaning
- workplace cleaning
- carpet refresh
- pest treatment

Semantic optimization rules:

- Keep entity use contextual, not repetitive.
- Match language to the page type.
- Use commercial hygiene and janitorial language for business pages.
- Use vacate, move-out, handover, and inspection language for rental pages.
- Use sanitation and disinfection only where the scope genuinely supports it.

## SECTION 9 - Technical SEO

Implement and maintain:

- LocalBusiness schema
- Service schema
- FAQ schema
- Breadcrumb schema

Use Review schema carefully:

- Review structured data is not a shortcut for local-business self-promotion. Google does not show rich results for self-serving reviews on LocalBusiness pages. Use testimonials for trust, but do not rely on self-serving review markup for visibility.

Core Web Vitals targets:

- LCP under 2.5 seconds
- CLS under 0.1
- INP under 200 milliseconds
- TTFB under 800 milliseconds

Performance priorities:

- convert large images to WebP or AVIF
- lazy load non-critical imagery
- reduce unnecessary JS on marketing pages
- preload only critical fonts and hero assets
- use strong browser caching and CDN delivery
- avoid popups or widgets that interrupt mobile interaction
