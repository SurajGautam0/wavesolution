📋 How to Use This Checklist

This comprehensive bond cleaning checklist is used by professional cleaners across Gold Coast. Simply work through each room, checking off items as you complete them. For best results, clean from top to bottom in each room.

💡 Pro Tip: Property managers are extremely thorough. Missing even small details like light switches or skirting boards can result in bond deductions. This checklist covers everything they check.

🍳 Kitchen

Clean oven inside and out including racks and glass
Degrease rangehood filters and wipe exterior
Clean stovetop including burners and knobs
Empty and clean refrigerator/freezer (defrost if needed)
Clean dishwasher filter and run empty cycle
Wipe down all cupboard doors inside and out
Clean drawers and remove crumbs
Clean sink and polish taps
Wipe splashback tiles and grout
Clean benchtops thoroughly
Sweep and mop floor including corners
Clean light fixtures and replace any dead bulbs
Wipe switches and power points
Remove any marks from walls
Clean skirting boards

🚿 Bathroom(s)

Scrub shower/bath including tiles, grout, and glass
Remove soap scum and limescale
Clean showerhead and remove mineral deposits
Polish all taps and fixtures
Deep clean toilet bowl, seat, and behind toilet
Clean bathroom sink and vanity
Wipe mirrors until streak-free
Clean inside cabinets and drawers
Remove mold from grout and silicone
Clean exhaust fan grill
Sweep and mop floor including behind door
Wipe down walls and remove marks
Clean light fixtures
Clean skirting boards

🛏️ Bedrooms

Vacuum carpets edge to edge
Steam clean carpets if required by lease
Clean inside all wardrobes/closets
Wipe wardrobe shelves and hanging rails
Clean mirrors on wardrobes
Dust ceiling fans and light fixtures
Wipe light switches and power points
Remove marks from walls
Clean windows inside and out
Wipe window sills and tracks
Dust/wipe blinds or clean curtains
Clean skirting boards
Clean air conditioning vents

🛋️ Living Room / Dining Room

Vacuum all carpets thoroughly
Mop hard floors
Clean all windows inside and out
Wipe window sills and tracks
Clean blinds or curtains
Dust ceiling fans
Clean light fixtures and replace bulbs
Wipe switches and power points
Remove marks from walls
Clean skirting boards
Vacuum/clean air vents
Clean sliding door tracks

🧺 Laundry

Clean washing machine inside and out
Clean dryer lint filter and drum
Wipe down laundry tub and taps
Clean cupboards inside and out
Sweep and mop floor
Clean exhaust fan if present
Wipe walls and remove marks
Clean skirting boards

🌳 Outdoor Areas

Sweep balcony/patio
Remove cobwebs from eaves and walls
Clean outdoor light fixtures
Sweep garage floor
Clean garage shelving
Remove all rubbish and recycling
Mow lawn if required
Weed garden beds
Clean mailbox

✅ Final Walkthrough

All personal belongings removed
All keys collected (mailbox, garage, etc.)
All rubbish removed from property
Check all bulbs are working
Check smoke alarms are clean
Close all windows and doors
Take photos/video of clean property
Forward cleaning receipts to property manager
